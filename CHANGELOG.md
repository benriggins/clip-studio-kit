# What's new in Clip Studio

## 2026-10-24: Instagram carousels post as stills
- A carousel now posts as its still slides (1080x1440, checked), with the caption in a POSTING.md and no audio in the
  files, because a video slide blocks adding music in the Instagram app. Reels still ship with music built in.

## 2026-10-22: cinematic carousels and video carousels
- Carousels learn what Instagram rewards: a sixth framework ("steal this" cheat sheets, built to be saved), slide 2
  written as a second hook, a last slide that asks for a comment keyword, share or save, and a reminder to add a
  music bed built into every video carousel (one bed cut across the slides, a new style each time), so
  nobody has to add a sound in the app.
- FYP / Explore carousels: 3:4 tall format by default, a profile-grid safe-zone check, five frameworks (stats &
  proof, contrarian, tutorial, problem + fix, before / after) and seven new looks: Notes app, hot take, analytics
  proof card, scrapbook with taped polaroids and a stamp, pop-up collage, blur-to-sharp reveal, seamless panorama.
- Six new carousel openers so no two posts look the same: text thread, lock-screen notifications, receipt, photo
  inside the word, before/after split, live lower third with a ticker.
- Five video-slide styles: push, whip (fast), type (messages pop in, the headline types on), rack focus, and
  cinemagraph (Grok animates the slide's photo). Replace 08 in your ChatGPT Project; prompt 13 picks them for you.
- Five cinematic carousel slides: a giant word behind the subject's head (auto cut-out), film slate, field-note callout,
  monument number behind the horizon, scoreboard.
- `carousel.py generate` makes carousel images with GPT Image 2.5; `carousel.py motion` turns every slide into a video
  slide; `carousel.py reel` cuts the carousel into one 9:16 Reel for the For You feed. ChatGPT prompt 13.

## 2026-10-21: the cinema engine
- One command, `tools/cinema.py`, turns a ChatGPT plan into a finished premium "business cinema" Reel or Short.
- Generated B-roll: GPT Image 2.5 first frames animated with Grok Imagine Video 1.5 (kie.ai), graded to match you.
- Glass UI cards, timers and check cards that land on your words; giant words that sit behind your head.
- New "cinema" captions (white, the key word big in italic), a faster pace, and music you can actually hear.
- ChatGPT Project: two new files (09 cinema playbook, 10 shot and motion library) and prompt 12. Add them to your Project.
- Audio is cleaned automatically on every take; Carousel Studio for Instagram and LinkedIn carousels.
